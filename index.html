anon@crossover ~ % man crossover

the fine print.

What's live, what's preview, and what it will never ask you for.

is this financial advice?

No. CROSSOVER shows where a coin's buys come from. A crossover is a signal, not a promise, and the signal log shows the misses too.

what's live and what's preview?

Live: the fingerprint list, the reader, the engine watching coins around the clock, the crossover rule and the signal log with prices after 15 min, 1 h and 4 h. Preview: the engine's own buys and its treasury. The log shows what it would buy; it doesn't trade yet.

how does it know a buy came from an app?

Apps and terminals leave traces inside the transaction: a fee sent to their wallet, their own program, a sponsor paying the gas, or a tip to a fast sender. CROSSOVER matches every buy against an open list of those addresses. It's an estimate, and buys it can't match are left out of the share.

why does app flow matter?

App users arrive later and in bigger numbers. Pump.fun's app alone does tens of millions in daily volume and passed Fomo, the biggest social trading app. When a coin's buys switch from terminals to apps, the crowd has found it.

do I need a wallet?

No. Reading is free and needs nothing. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere.

will crossover DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
